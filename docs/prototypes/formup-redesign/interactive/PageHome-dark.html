QUESTIONS

Before you start

Does my client pay for the app?

No. The mobile app is free for your clients.

How do I bring my clients over?

Invite them by email from the portal. They install the app and land straight in your client list.

Will my client see unfinished weeks?

No. Clients only see the weeks you publish. Drafts stay hidden until you are ready.

Can a trainer and a nutritionist share a client?

Yes. Each of you owns your own plan, and the client sees both in one app.

Can I reuse plans across clients?

Yes. Save sessions, meals or a whole plan as a template and give it to the next client in one click.

Which languages does Form Up speak?

Czech, English and German — in the web portal and in the mobile app.
