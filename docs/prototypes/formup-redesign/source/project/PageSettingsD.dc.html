Removing a role hides it from search and stops new work in it: no new plans or templates, and no new clients for it. Danger zone

Actions that end your work on Form Up.

Disable coach account

Removes all your roles and stops billing when your current subscription ends. Your data stays, read-only.

Delete account

Permanently erases your account, plans, templates and messages. Clients lose access to what you shared. This can't be undone.
