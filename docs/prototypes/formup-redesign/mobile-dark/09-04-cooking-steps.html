Chicken rice bowl

How to make it

For 3 portionsStep 2 of 5 · about 25 min
Cook the riceCook enough rice for 690 g cooked — about 240 g dry in 600 ml water.
2
Prepare the chickenCut 450 g chicken breast into strips. Season with salt and pepper.
3
FryHeat 3 tbsp olive oil in a pan. Fry the chicken until golden.
4
Steam the broccoliSteam 450 g broccoli until bright green and tender.
5
ServeSplit rice, chicken and broccoli into 3 bowls. Drizzle each with 1 tsp soy sauce.
